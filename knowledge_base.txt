--- DIETARY INFORMATION ---
Vegetarian options: Veggie Burger, Side Salad (without parmesan for vegan), Sweet Potato Fries, Classic Fries, Onion Rings.
Vegan options: Veggie Burger (no chipotle mayo — sub mustard), Side Salad (no parmesan, vinaigrette dressing), Classic Fries, Sweet Potato Fries.
Gluten-free: Our Classic Fries and Sweet Potato Fries are gluten-free. All burgers can be served lettuce-wrapped instead of a bun — just ask. Our buns, chicken sandwich breading, and onion rings contain gluten.
Dairy-free: Ask to remove cheese from any burger. Our Classic Fries and most sides are dairy-free. Shakes contain dairy.
Nut allergies: We do not use tree nuts or peanuts in any of our recipes. However, our kitchen handles shared equipment, so cross-contamination is possible. Please inform staff of severe allergies.

--- ALLERGEN SUMMARY ---
All items are prepared in a shared kitchen that handles gluten (wheat), dairy, and eggs.
Cross-contamination with allergens is possible.
If you have a severe food allergy, please speak with a staff member before ordering.

--- ORDERING AND CUSTOMIZATION ---
We welcome customizations. Common requests:
- No onions, no pickles, no lettuce, no tomato — no problem.
- Extra sauce — just ask.
- Double patty — add $2.50 to any burger.
- Substitute sweet potato fries for regular fries — add $0.50.
- Lettuce wrap instead of bun — no extra charge.
- Make it a combo — add medium fries and medium drink for a discounted bundle price.

--- FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ---
Q: Do you have Wi-Fi?
A: Yes, free Wi-Fi is available. Ask at the counter for the password.

Q: Do you take reservations?
A: We do not take reservations. Seating is first-come, first-served. For large groups (8+), call ahead and we will do our best to have space ready.

Q: Is there outdoor seating?
A: Yes, we have a covered patio with 8 tables, available spring through fall weather permitting.

Q: Do you deliver?
A: Yes, we are available on DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. You can also order directly through our website for pickup.

Q: How spicy is the Nashville Hot Chicken?
A: It has a genuine kick — comparable to a medium-hot buffalo wing. We have a mild version if you want the flavor without the full heat. Ask for "mild Nashville" when ordering.

Q: Can I get the Veggie Burger without the bun?
A: Absolutely — lettuce wrap at no extra charge.

Q: Do you have a kids menu?
A: Yes, the Kids Burger ($5.49) comes with a small fry and juice box. We can also do a smaller portion of the grilled cheese on request.

Q: What's the wait time?
A: Typical wait is 5 to 10 minutes during normal hours. During peak lunch (12–1pm) and dinner (6–8pm) times, expect 10 to 15 minutes. We will always give you an honest estimate at the counter.

Q: Can I call in an order ahead of time?
A: Yes, call (555) 123-4567 and we will have it ready when you arrive. Payment can be made over the phone or at pickup.
